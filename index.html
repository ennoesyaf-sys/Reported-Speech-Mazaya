Reported Speech

Mulai di sini

Reported speech (kalimat tidak langsung) dipakai untuk menceritakan ulang apa yang dikatakan orang lain, tanpa mengutip kata-katanya persis. Makna sama, bentuk berbeda.

Direct speechThen, he said, “Catch the fish.”
Reported speechThen, he asked me to catch the fish.

Cara berpikir 4 langkah

  1. Kenali jenisnya. Tidak ada subjek (Call me…, Don’t…)? Itu perintah/permintaan. Ada subjek dan titik? Pernyataan. Ada tanda tanya? Pertanyaan.
  2. Pilih polanya. Setiap jenis punya pola sendiri (lihat di bawah).
  3. Mundur satu langkah. Tense bergeser ke masa lampau, karena ucapan aslinya sudah lewat.
  4. Ganti pelaku dan waktu. Kata ganti (I → he/she), keterangan waktu/tempat (tomorrow → the next day, here → there).

Tiga pola utama

JenisPolaContoh
Perintah / permintaanS + told/asked + O + (not) to + V1She told us not to do that.
PernyataanS + said (that) + klausa mundur satu tenseShe said that she always drank coffee.
PertanyaanS + asked + if/whether atau kata tanya + S + VHe asked me what time the train left.

Tips kata ganti

Tanyakan dua hal: siapa yang bicara dan siapa yang melaporkan. “my” milik si pembicara jadi his/her (kecuali pembicaranya adalah kamu sendiri). “me” di perintah biasanya jadi him/her, sedangkan “you” yang dilapori jadi me/him/her sesuai penerima.

Halaman dari buku yang difoto berhenti di awal Activity 7 (adjective clause), jadi latihan ini mencakup Activity 2, 4, dan 6.

Rangkuman dan cek cepat

Kesalahan yang paling sering muncul

SalahBenar
He told me don’t touch it.He told me not to touch it.
She asked me do I work from home.She asked me if I worked from home.
He asked where did she go.He asked where she went.
She said me that she was tired.She told me that she was tired. / She said that she was tired.
He asked me to please come.He asked me to come. (“please” dibuang)
He said he will come tomorrow.He said he would come the next day.

Kapan tense tidak perlu mundur?

Kalau kata kerja pelapor masih present (He says he has missed the train), atau kalau isinya masih benar sekarang (We explained that it is very difficult to find our house). Modal might, could, would, should, ought to tidak berubah. Sedangkan can → could, will → would, may → might.
